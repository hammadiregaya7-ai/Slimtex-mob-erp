# Cost & Inventory Management System (ERP-lite) — Textile Manufacturing & Wholesale

An enterprise-grade, responsive Android ERP-lite application engineered for textile mills, garment manufacturers, wholesalers, and fashion boutiques. The system tracks raw materials (fabrics in meters, yarn in kg, trims), production orders, Bill of Materials (BOM), cost sheets with labor and overhead, double-entry financial accounting (Chart of Accounts, Journal Entries), and commercial sales & purchase workflows with real-time cloud synchronization.

## 1. Overview & Core Concept

### What It Does
**Textile ERP-lite** delivers end-to-end operational and financial control for textile businesses:
1. **Executive Dashboard**: Real-time snapshot of business health (Total Revenue, Gross Cost of Goods Sold, Net Accounts Receivable, Accounts Payable, Inventory Valuation, and Active Production Orders).
2. **Double-Entry Chart of Accounts (COA)**: Standard accounting structure (Assets, Liabilities, Equity, Revenue, Expense) with automated double-entry ledger postings generated by inventory stock movements, sales invoices, and supplier purchases.
3. **Inventory & Warehouse Tracking**: Multi-unit inventory for raw materials (meters, yards, kg, spools, units) and finished apparel (garments, boxed goods). Complete stock-in, stock-out, and stock adjustment audit trails.
4. **Cost Accounting & Production Orders**: Bill of Materials (BOM) management, production batch creation, allocation of raw materials, tracking of labor hours and factory overheads, automated cost sheet generation (Cost Per Unit, Material Variance), and one-click production completion that auto-decrements raw materials and stocks in finished goods.
5. **Commercial Purchasing & Invoicing**: Supplier Purchase Orders (POs) with Accounts Payable tracking; Customer Commercial Invoices with Accounts Receivable tracking and payment settlement.

### Target Audience & Persona
- **Factory Managers & Production Supervisors**: Need quick batch tracking, raw material requisition, and unit cost calculation.
- **Textile Traders & Wholesalers**: Need inventory valuation by roll/lot and clear customer/vendor debit/credit balances.
- **Boutique Owners & Designers**: Need streamlined order creation, trim tracking, and lightweight bookkeeping on phone or tablet.

### Key Value
Eliminates fragmented spreadsheets and manual paper logs by combining inventory control, manufacturing cost analysis, and double-entry accounting into a single responsive Android app.

## 3. Key Product Decisions & Trade-Offs

### Decision 1: Combined Inventory & Double-Entry Accounting Engine
- **Chosen Approach**: Inventory movements (receiving, manufacturing consumption, finished goods production, sales dispatch) directly create corresponding balanced Journal Entries in the Chart of Accounts.
  - *Purchasing Fabric*: Debit `1200 Inventory - Raw Materials`, Credit `2000 Accounts Payable`.
  - *Issuing to Production*: Debit `1300 Work in Progress (WIP)`, Credit `1200 Inventory - Raw Materials`.
  - *Completing Production*: Debit `1400 Inventory - Finished Goods`, Credit `1300 Work in Progress (WIP)` + `5100 Direct Labor Applied` + `5200 Factory Overhead Applied`.
  - *Commercial Sale*: Debit `1100 Accounts Receivable`, Credit `4000 Sales Revenue`; Debit `5000 Cost of Goods Sold`, Credit `1400 Inventory - Finished Goods`.
- **Why**: Gives textile business owners real-time financial statements without requiring manual reconciliations or separate accounting software.
- **Alternatives Considered**: Isolated inventory tracking without journal entries was rejected because textile businesses require accurate COGS and margin calculations to stay profitable.

### Decision 2: Cloud Database Architecture (Firebase Firestore)
- **Chosen Approach**: Firestore collections partitioned per authenticated user (`users/{userId}/...`), keeping records safe and multi-device synchronized.
- **Why**: Guarantees secure multi-device synchronization across warehouse floor tablets and office management phones, with offline write queuing built into the Firestore Android SDK.
- **Security Rule Enforcement**: Strict owner-bound rules (`request.auth.uid == userId`) with data model validation on every write.

### Data Model & Firestore Schema

1. **`items` (Inventory Catalog)**
   - `id`: Document ID
   - `code`: SKU / Barcode (e.g. `RM-COT-01`, `FG-SHT-BLK`)
   - `name`: String (e.g. "100% Combed Cotton Fabric 60s", "Classic Linen Shirt")
   - `category`: `"RAW_MATERIAL" | "FINISHED_GOOD" | "TRIM"`
   - `unit`: `"METERS" | "KG" | "PIECES" | "ROLLS" | "YARDS"`
   - `currentStock`: Double (e.g. 1450.5 meters)
   - `reorderLevel`: Double (e.g. 200.0)
   - `averageCost`: Double (unit purchase / production cost)
   - `sellingPrice`: Double (for finished goods)
   - `storageLocation`: String (e.g. "Aisle 3 - Rack B")

2. **`production_orders` (Batches & Cost Sheets)**
   - `id`: Document ID
   - `batchNumber`: String (e.g. `BAT-2026-081`)
   - `finishedItemId`: String
   - `finishedItemName`: String
   - `targetQuantity`: Double (e.g. 500 shirts)
   - `status`: `"PLANNED" | "IN_PROGRESS" | "COMPLETED" | "CANCELLED"`
   - `materials`: List of BOM line items (`itemId`, `name`, `quantityRequired`, `unitCost`, `totalCost`)
   - `directLaborCost`: Double
   - `overheadCost`: Double
   - `totalBatchCost`: Double
   - `unitProductionCost`: Double
   - `createdAt`: Timestamp
   - `completedAt`: Timestamp?

3. **`stock_transactions` (Inventory Audit Log)**
   - `id`: Document ID
   - `itemId`: String
   - `itemName`: String
   - `type`: `"PURCHASE_RECEIPT" | "PRODUCTION_CONSUMPTION" | "PRODUCTION_OUTPUT" | "SALES_DISPATCH" | "ADJUSTMENT"`
   - `quantityDelta`: Double (positive or negative)
   - `unitCost`: Double
   - `referenceNumber`: String (PO #, Batch #, or Invoice #)
   - `timestamp`: Timestamp

4. **`accounts` (Chart of Accounts)**
   - `id`: Document ID
   - `accountCode`: String (`1010 Cash`, `1200 Raw Materials`, `1400 Finished Goods`, `2000 AP`, `4000 Sales`, `5000 COGS`)
   - `accountName`: String
   - `type`: `"ASSET" | "LIABILITY" | "EQUITY" | "REVENUE" | "EXPENSE"`
   - `currentBalance`: Double

5. **`journal_entries` (Double-Entry General Ledger)**
   - `id`: Document ID
   - `entryNumber`: String
   - `date`: Timestamp
   - `memo`: String
   - `lines`: List of `{ accountCode, accountName, debit, credit }` (enforcing sum(debit) == sum(credit))

6. **`commercial_orders` (POs and Invoices)**
   - `id`: Document ID
   - `type`: `"PURCHASE_ORDER" | "SALES_INVOICE"`
   - `orderNumber`: String (`PO-1029`, `INV-8032`)
   - `partnerName`: String (Supplier or Customer)
   - `totalAmount`: Double
   - `status`: `"DRAFT" | "CONFIRMED" | "FULFILLED" | "PAID"`
   - `items`: List of order line items

## 5. Implementation Steps & Milestones

1. **Backend Provisioning & Security Rules**:
   - Provision Firebase Firestore & Authentication via `ProvisionFirebase`.
   - Author `firebase-blueprint.json` and strict `firestore.rules`.
   - Validate security rules via unit and integration tests, then deploy via `DeployRules`.
2. **Data & Accounting Engine Layer**:
   - Implement type-safe Kotlin models and `ErpRepository`.
   - Implement `DoubleEntryEngine` for auto-balancing ledger entries and `CostSheetCalculator`.
   - Pre-seed realistic textile manufacturing data (fabrics, yarn, trims, finished apparel, Chart of Accounts).
3. **Adaptive UI Shell & Navigation**:
   - Create responsive layouts supporting both Tablet (`NavigationRail` + two-pane details) and Mobile (`NavigationBar` + modal sheets).
   - Theme configuration with M3 ColorScheme (dark/light), Material ripples, and typography.
4. **Core Functional Modules**:
   - **Dashboard**: Metric cards, revenue/expense trends, inventory valuation, active batch status, and low-stock alert drawer.
   - **Inventory Management**: Searchable catalog, category tabs, stock-in/out dialogs, and roll/lot tracking.
   - **Production & Costing**: Production batch builder, live BOM cost calculator, labor/overhead inputs, and one-click completion.
   - **Accounting & Commercial**: Chart of Accounts viewer, General Ledger journal history, and Purchase Orders / Invoices with balance settlement.
5. **Compilation & Polish**:
   - Verify build and tests via `compile_applet`.
   - Validate smooth transitions, empty states, and accessibility touch targets (>= 48dp).
